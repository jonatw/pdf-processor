Frequently Asked Questions

100% Safe. We cannot see your files.
Think of this website like a calculator on your phone. Even though you open it in a browser, everything happens right there on your device. Your file never leaves your computer or phone. It is not uploaded to any "cloud" or server. It stays with you the entire time.

You can turn off your internet!
Once this page is loaded, you can switch on "Airplane Mode" or disconnect your Wi-Fi. The tool will still work perfectly. This proves that it doesn't need to send your file anywhere to process it.

No limit.
You can try any file size. However, because your own device is doing the hard work (not a supercomputer), very large files might make your browser run slow or your phone get a bit warm. This is normal!

This is a special kind of website called a "Web App" that can be installed like a regular app!

  • On iPhone (using Safari): Tap the "Share" button (), then find and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  • On Android (using Chrome): Tap the three dots menu (), then tap "Add to Home screen".
It will then show up as an icon on your phone's home screen, just like any other app.
